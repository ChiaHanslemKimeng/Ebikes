1. The 30-Day In-Home Trial Policy

We want you to feel 100% confident in your choice. From the day your Surron bicycle is delivered to your doorstep, you have a full 30-day window to test it on your commute, trails, and weekend getaways.

If you decide the bicycle is not right for you, contact us within 30 days of delivery to initiate a return.

2. Return Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for a refund under our 30-Day Trial:

  • Odometer Reading: The digital LCD display odometer must show fewer than 20.0 miles total recorded distance.
  • Physical Condition: The bicycle must be clean, free from frame dents, deep scratches, tire gouges, or mechanical alterations.
  • Complete Contents: The original packaging, charger, two battery keys, toolkit, reflector set, and owner's manuals must be repacked in the original box.
  • Spare Parts & Electronics: Replacement controllers, throttles, and wiring components must not have cut or spliced wires.

3. Step-by-Step Return Process (RMA)

Step 1 Request RMA Email support@surronbikesandparts.shop with your order number.
Step 2 Prepaid Label We send you a prepaid freight shipping bill of lading.
Step 3 Carrier Pickup FedEx or freight carrier collects the sealed box from your door.
Step 4 Fast Refund Inspected within 48h; funds returned to your original payment card.

Note: A standard $150 freight return deduction is subtracted from refunds on complete bicycles to cover the carrier hazardous ground transport cost, unless the return is due to an unresolvable factory defect.

5. Crash Replacement Assistance Program

Accidents happen. If your Surron is damaged in a traffic incident or mountain crash that is not covered by factory warranty, we offer registered owners a 30% discount off standard MSRP on replacement frames, wheels, motors, and structural components to get you back on the road safely.

6. How to Submit a Warranty Claim

To begin a warranty claim, simply email support@surronbikesandparts.shop with:

  1. Your original 6-digit Surron Order Number.
  2. The bike serial number (permanently engraved beneath the bottom bracket shell).
  3. A clear 15-second video or 2–3 photos displaying the issue.

Our master mechanics will review the diagnostic within 24 business hours and expedite replacement parts directly to your address with complimentary technical installation guidance.
