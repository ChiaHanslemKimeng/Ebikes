How Our Service Depot Works

Getting your electric bike serviced or repaired is transparent, fast, and stress-free.

1
Submit Online Request

Fill out our quick service booking form with your bike model, symptoms, or error codes. You will receive a diagnostic ticket number within 2 hours.

2
Depot Drop-Off or Ship

Drop off your bike or battery at one of our regional hub locations, or request a prepaid insured freight shipping carton right to your front door.

3
Certified Diagnostic & Repair

Our certified mechanics perform multi-point diagnostics. We send you an itemized report before performing any repair work with genuine OEM parts.

4
Road Test & Return

Every serviced bike undergoes a strict 5-mile real-world dynamometer and road test before being cleaned and safely returned to your garage.
