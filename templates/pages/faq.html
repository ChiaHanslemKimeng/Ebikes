Batteries, Real-World Range & Charging

We exclusively use Grade-A certified Samsung (35E and 50E 21700) and LG Chem NMC lithium-ion cells. Each battery pack features an intelligent multi-channel Battery Management System (BMS) with thermal cut-offs, cell-level over-charge protection, and individual nickel-plate fusing. Our packs are rated for 800 to 1,000 complete charge cycles before dropping to 70% capacity—translating to roughly 5 to 7 years of typical commuter use.

Never charge a lithium-ion battery when its temperature is below freezing (32°F / 0°C), as this can cause irreversible lithium plating. If storing your bike for more than 30 days during winter:
  • Remove the battery key and store the pack indoors at room temperature (50°F–70°F / 10°C–21°C).
  • Store the pack at 50% to 70% state of charge (approx. 3 out of 4 LED bars). Do not store at 100% or 0%.
  • Top off the charge for 30 minutes every 60 days to prevent parasitic sleep-mode drainage.

Our advertised range figures (e.g. 50–70 miles on the Surron Commuter Pro) are measured with a 170-pound rider on level paved terrain using Pedal Assist Level 2 at approximately 15 mph. In throttle-only mode or when tackling steep continuous 12%+ alpine climbs, range will be closer to 30–35 miles. Using eco assist modes on flat commutes can yield over 75 miles per charge.

Motors, Sensors & All-Weather Riding

Cadence Sensors act like an on/off switch: once the cranks start spinning, the motor delivers a predetermined power level regardless of how hard you press. Torque Sensors measure the exact physical force (strain gauge) you apply to the pedals in real time. If you pedal gently, the motor provides whisper-quiet subtle assistance; if you push hard up a steep hill, the motor instantly surges with full torque. All Surron Pro and Mountain models use precision bilateral torque sensors for an intuitive, natural cycling feel.

Yes. All Surron electrical components, motors, displays, and wiring harnesses carry an IP65 water resistance rating. Riding in steady rain, splashing through street puddles, or riding on wet snow will not damage the system. However, you should never submerge the bike in deep water or use high-pressure automotive pressure washers, which can force water past bearing seals. Wipe the frame down with a damp cloth and mild soap.

E-Bike Classifications & Street Legality

Surron models are configured out of the box as Class 2 electric bicycles (20 mph max assist with throttle and pedal assist), making them 100% legal on multi-use bike paths, lanes, and municipal streets without requiring a driver's license, vehicle registration, or insurance in almost all US states and Canadian provinces. Our digital LCD displays allow riders to switch into Class 3 mode (28 mph pedal assist) where legally permitted.

In the United States and Canada, Class 1 and Class 2 e-bikes do not require a driver's license or motor vehicle registration. Standard bicycle helmets with CPSC certification are strongly recommended for all riders and legally mandatory for riders under 18 (and for all Class 3 riders in states like California, Washington, and New York). For speeds above 20 mph, we recommend an NTA-8776 certified e-bike helmet.
