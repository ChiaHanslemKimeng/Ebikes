The Surron Genesis

From Silicon Valley Garage to Nationwide Fleet

Founded in 2021 by a dedicated team of automotive electrical engineers and avid bicycle commuters, Surron was created to resolve a fundamental market frustration: e-bikes were either overpriced luxury status symbols locked down with proprietary software, or cheap disposable imports with unreliable battery packs and zero replacement parts.

We chose a radically different path: open-architecture, modular electric bicycles engineered with certified Grade-A Samsung and LG lithium cells, high-torque Bafang drivetrains, and aerospace-grade 6061 hydroformed aluminum frames. Every bike we manufacture can be serviced, upgraded, and repaired with standard tools for decades of dependable riding.

Our Evolution

Milestones in Electric Innovation

How a modest prototype evolved into North America's trusted open-platform electric mobility brand.

2021 • Origin
Prototype Zero & Inception

Engineered our first hydroformed aluminum commuter frame in a British Columbia workshop. Replaced generic lead acid components with bespoke 48V Samsung 18650 packs.

2022 • Growth
Surron Commuter Pro Launch

Introduced the flagship Commuter Pro with integrated Bafang 750W hub motor and color LCD. Surpassed 2,500 active riders within the first 6 months of nationwide launch.

2023 • Quality
Dedicated Battery Test Facility

Commissioned an in-house thermal dynamics and battery impedance laboratory. Achieved UL 2849 full-system electrical safety certification across all models.

2024 • Expansion
Open Parts Catalog & Right to Repair

Launched our open OEM spare parts catalog with complete pinout wiring diagrams and step-by-step video repair tutorials for independent mechanics.

2025 • Impact
15,000 Riders & Fleet Solutions

Crossed 15,000 electric bicycles deployed. Partnered with regional courier and campus security programs with heavy-duty commercial cargo configurations.

2026 • Present
Smart IoT Telematics & Solid-State

Pioneering smart Bluetooth battery diagnostics, over-the-air motor torque curve mapping, and initial solid-state cell endurance prototyping.

Engineering Excellence

Our 4 Pillars of Mechanical Durability

We subject every frame, motor batch, and battery pack to rigorous mechanical stress tests before it reaches your door.

Cell Thermal Runaway Protection

Every pack features individual cell nickel-plated fuse links, pyrophoric barriers, and dual thermistor sensors that shut down power within 2ms of abnormal thermal spikes.

200,000 Cycle ISO Stress Tests

Our 6061 hydroformed frames undergo pneumatic horizontal fatigue tests simulating 10 years of aggressive urban potholes and rocky mountain drops with zero weld fatigue.

IP65 All-Weather Sealing

Motor casings, controller housings, and quick-disconnect Julet connectors are gasketed to IP65 standards, allowing confident riding through heavy rainstorms, snow, and mud.

Complete Right-to-Repair Open Specs

No proprietary locking software. We publish full electrical pinout tables and provide accessible spare parts so you or your local bike shop can repair any part anytime.

Environmental Responsibility

100% Closed-Loop Battery Recycling

At Surron, environmental stewardship does not stop at zero tailpipe emissions. In official partnership with Call2Recycle®, we operate a nationwide battery recovery initiative. When your battery pack eventually completes its multi-year operational lifecycle, you can ship it to any Surron service depot for free, closed-loop processing.

Over 95% of cobalt, nickel, and copper are recovered and recycled back into clean energy supply chains, ensuring zero lithium cells end up in municipal landfills.

Our Green Operations Pledge
  • Zero-Plastic Shipping: 100% recycled biodegradable honeycomb cardboard internal bracing replaces harmful styrofoam blocks.
  • Solar Powered Assembly: Our Vancouver, BC engineering and distribution facility draws over 80% of its electricity from a 120kW rooftop solar array.
  • Free End-of-Life Disposal: Complimentary return shipping labels provided for spent battery packs to ensure certified metallurgical recycling.
